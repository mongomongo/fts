What the columns mean
  • Column sets (like the FTS tool): Goal markets, Match odds, All, or your own: pick columns under Columns and press Save custom. Click a column heading to sort by it (again to reverse). The match columns stay put when you scroll sideways.
  • Filters (like the FTS tool): add as many as you like, each “column – operator – value”, joined with And or Or. For example: League is not Dutch Eerste Divisie, XG > 2, O2.5 value % ≥ 5, Ticked is true. They're remembered in this browser.
  • Ticks: tick the games you're watching (the box at the start of each row), then use Ticked only or a Ticked is true filter, as Ian does with his imported games.
  • Goals: the scoring team is coloured green for a minute when a goal comes in (change it with Goal flash). Goal times are under each team: ⚽ 23' 67'p (p = penalty, og = own goal, ~ = minute estimated until SofaScore confirms it).
  • XG: FTS's modelled total goals before kick-off (NewModel).
  • European games (Champions League, Europa League, Conference League): FTS doesn't cover them, so they come from Betfair's own fixture list through the price log. They get the live stats, Betfair prices, value squares (our fair price fitted to Betfair's prices at the off) and the setups, but none of FTS's columns (XG, O2.5 move, trading entries, league angles, half-time cells). The cards flag needs 5 finished games in the competition first. Our fair-price model was fitted on FTS's league games and hasn't been checked on European ones yet.
  • xG H / xG A: live expected goals for each side (big), with expected goals on target underneath (small).
  • Momentum: SofaScore attack momentum, one bar a minute. Blue above the line is home pressure, orange below is away. The faint line marks half-time. Markers show what happened when, home above the chart, away below: ● goal, yellow / red card, teal flag = corner. Hover (or open the game) for the minute and team. Goals and cards come from SofaScore's match events at the real minute; SofaScore doesn't give corner times, so a corner is placed between the two stats refreshes either side of it (give or take a couple of minutes, shown faded), and corners taken before the board found the game aren't timed.
  • Shots: total shots (big) and shots on target (small).
  • Value squares (games in play): the top number is our fair price, the bottom one the latest Betfair price from your in-play log. Value = (Betfair − fair) ÷ Betfair × 100, the same sum as the FTS tool. Green = value backing, red = none; the deeper the colour, the bigger the gap. The draw uses the lay price and is scored from the lay side (green = laying the draw is value). O0.5–O4.5 and FH O0.5 / O1.5 are the goal lines; a line that's already gone is blank. A crossed-out Betfair price is over 3 minutes old or was taken just before a goal, so no value is worked out from it.
  • How our fair price is made: each side's expected goals are fitted to the Betfair prices at the off (else the 7am prices, else NewModel xG). Then every remaining minute is played out with how goals really arrive (more after half-time, lots in added time) and how the score changes things (the side behind late scores more). Live xG nudges it a little. Red cards adjust it by who was sent off: the favourite down to 10 scores 35% less and the underdog 70% more; the underdog down to 10 scores 50% less and the favourite 40% more (fitted 18 Oct on 285 games with a sending-off, StatsBomb 2015/16). Fitted and checked on FTS's results file: 47,924 games since 2021.
  • How much to trust it: on 10,433 games from this season (not used to fit it), Betfair's half-time prices were slightly more accurate than ours, and backing every green goal square at half-time made no reliable profit. So a few % of value is mostly noise. Every reading is saved so we can test it on our own games.
  • O2.5 move: Over 2.5 at 7am → the last price before kick-off (green = shortened). Ian picks games where it has come in.
  • Setups (the Setups bar): the four moments worth acting on, each one a price and the game agreeing. Pick one to see only those games (the bar shows how many there are now, and switches to the columns it's about); its blue ★ chip in Flags says what to back and why. Goal coming = 20–35' or 55–75', both of the next two goal lines green and one 5%+ at 1.7 or bigger (Ian's rule, within the minutes where it makes sense). Going dead = from 30' or at half-time, Betfair's Under on the next goal line is 5%+ bigger than our fair Under, after allowing for our model's usual lean. Our model rates more goals a little lower than Betfair does (0.12–0.27 on the log-odds scale in 2,393 live readings), and on a long Under like 0-0 at 30' that lean alone looks like 10–16% value. So the board first moves our goal chance 0.2 towards Betfair, then asks for 5% on top. Corners: team = the corners flag below. Cards = cards brewing and the referee isn't lenient (“+ strict ref” when they're strict). After a sending-off there are no Goal coming / Going dead setups. The red-card adjustment was refitted on 18 Oct (the old one rated goals and the side with 11 men too low), but only on one season of 285 games, so the setups stay off there until the saved readings show the new prices hold up against Betfair. The goal setups are price-only on purpose: tested on 1,550 games played minute by minute (18 Oct), the goals flags added nothing beyond our fair price, so they don't decide anything. A setup is a reason to look, not an order: stake small until the saved readings show which make money. Alerts: setups beeps when one lines up (again after each goal).
  • Flags: the goals ones (Fav behind, xG > goals, Quiet) are context only. On 1,550 games played minute by minute (StatsBomb 2015/16, with the market's pre-match prices) the goals came at the rate our fair price already gave: xG > goals 69.6% another goal vs 68.4% expected, Quiet 83.8% vs 83.5%, and a favourite behind but on top won less often than expected (12.8% vs 16.2%). The corners and cards ones are tested and do say something: see below. ★ Goals value = both of the next two goal squares green, one 5%+ at 1.7 or bigger (Ian's rule). Fav behind, on top = the favourite is losing after 15' but leads xG by 0.4+ (or shots by 4+) and isn't behind on shots on target. xG > goals = after 20', total xG at least 0.9 more than the goals. Quiet = after 30', total xG under 0.35 and at most one shot on target.
  • Corners: corners so far, and underneath the corners each side is expected to win in the next 10 minutes (a normal side: about 0.5 in the first half, 0.55 in the second, 0.63 just before half-time). It comes from what drove corners in 1,551 games with every event logged (StatsBomb's free data: 2015/16 Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Ligue 1): who has had the ball and the box touches over the last 10 minutes, the side's corner rate so far, and the score (the side behind wins about 11% more, the side ahead about 17% fewer). xG and shots added nothing once possession and box touches were known. Corners: team flag = that side is expected to win at least 1.35× what a normal side would at that stage of the game (about 0.7 corners in the first half, 0.75 in the second, 0.85 just before half-time, when added time makes corners come thicker). That's about 1 side in 12 at any stage. In leagues the model wasn't fitted on, those sides won 1.41–1.50× the normal corners for that stage, and at least one in the next 10 minutes 50–53% of the time against 39–40%. Pressure moves corners to the side pressing; the match total barely changes (1.1–1.2×), so it says more about who wins the next corner than about total corner lines.
  • Cards: yellow (red) cards, and underneath the fouls with × = fouls so far against what's normal by now in this league. In the same games, fouls were the only live sign of cards to come: a game 25%+ over the league's fouls with one goal or less in it went on to 1.16× the normal yellows from 60' and 1.38× from 70'. Cards already shown said nothing about cards still to come, and a 2-goal gap cut them by about 18%. Cards brewing flag = from 55', fouls 1.25× the league norm or more, goal gap 0 or 1. League norms start from football-data.co.uk 2025/26 for the big five and are learned from the board's own finished games for every other league (5 games before the flag is used).
  • Referee: the referee's yellow cards a game over their career (SofaScore), and × against the other referees the board has seen in that league (shrunk towards the league average, so a referee with few games doesn't stand out by luck). Strict ref = ×1.10 or more, Lenient ref = ×0.90 or less (needs 20+ games and 3 other referees seen in the league). Tested on Premier League games 2005–2026 using only each referee's earlier games: strict referees gave 13% more yellows (5+ yellows 30% of games vs 26%), lenient ones 20% fewer (5+ yellows 14%). Bookmakers and Betfair know who the referee is too, so this is context, not an edge on its own.
  • Trading (column set Trading): several kinds of entry, each flagged only while its moment is on: 3 minutes for the card's and FTS's entries (their minimum is the price at that minute, so it goes stale), 5 for the half-time and league-angle ones. A ✓ flag means Betfair is at or above the minimum price. An outlined flag means the moment is on but the price is below the minimum: wait, or skip it.
    • Card: Ian's Trading Card for today, with GO / SKIP from the card's check before kick-off. A skipped trade gets no in-play flags. In play the card's own minimum prices are used: O1.5 drip at 20', 27' and 35' (0-0 only), FH goal at 25' (0-0), late goal at 70', 75' and 80' (while there's been no goal since 70'). Green up: X lead = a Lead & Green (or featured) team has gone ahead. Take your liability out = the first goal came after the drip's first entry. The card's record so far counts trades that “landed”, not profit.
    • FTS entries: FTS's trading-entry columns from the NewModel file, for every game they cover: 0-0 at 25' (FH goal), goal by 10' (2nd FH goal), 0-0 at 20' (a goal), goal after 70' and after 80'. Each chance becomes FTS's minimum price (100 ÷ chance). They're well calibrated, so they're fair prices, not an edge, and they picked games no better than the pre-match price did. So the board only flags one when Betfair beats both FTS's minimum and our own fair price. The Entry column shows them all.
    • HT: no more goals: at half-time, in one of 28 league × goals-at-HT combinations where the half-time market has over-priced second-half goals, back Under (goals + 0.5). Picked on past seasons and bet on the next one, it made +5.0% on 3,705 bets in 2024–26 (with shuffled leagues: −1.1%, p ≈ 0.005). This season so far it's −10.9% on 445 bets, about 1.5 standard deviations below what's expected, which is still within normal swings. The price log is still checking that FTS's half-time price can really be had, so treat it as a candidate and stake small (0.25–0.5pt). The minimum shown is the break-even price at that combination's past rate.
    • Angle (league angles): moments where one league has beaten the board's own league-blind price since 2021, and kept doing so in the later seasons. Brazil 0-0 at 25' and Brazil / Poland 0-0 at 35' (first-half goal), a 1-goal lead at 70' (another goal: Bundesliga, Poland, Swiss, Ligue 1, Austria, Eredivisie, Premier League; no more goals: J-League, Championship), the Championship after 80' (no more goals), and the Bundesliga after 60' (2+ more goals). The angle's price is our chance plus that league's effect, shrunk towards zero so a small league doesn't stand out by luck. The flag shows only when Betfair is at or above it. Where we could check against real prices (at half-time), Betfair already allowed for about half of each league effect, so expect less than the history suggests. Each one is checked against the logged Betfair prices before it's trusted.
    • Alerts: choose Alerts: ticked games or all games for a beep (and a browser notification, if you allow it) when a flag turns ✓ or a green-up comes, or Alerts: setups to beep only when one of the four setups lines up. Keep the page open. Every entry is saved, so we can check later whether they made money.
  • xG and xG on target are only available in leagues where SofaScore has them. Elsewhere you still get shots, shots on target and momentum.
