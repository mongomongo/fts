What the columns mean
  • Pre xG: FTS's modelled total goals before kick-off (NewModel).
  • xG H / xG A: live expected goals for each side (big), with expected goals on target underneath (small). xG on target only counts shots that hit the target, weighted by where they were going, so it shows how hard each keeper is being worked.
  • Momentum: SofaScore attack momentum, one bar a minute. Blue above the line is home pressure, orange below is away. The faint line marks half-time.
  • Shots: total shots (big) and shots on target (small).
  • Home / Draw / Away: Betfair back prices from your in-play price log (small: the price at the off, or 7am). Goals: the Over line just above the current score, plus first-half Over 0.5 in the first half.
  • Flags are rules of thumb, not tested yet: Fav behind, on top = the favourite is losing after 15' but leads xG by 0.4+ (or shots by 4+) and isn't behind on shots on target. xG > goals = after 20', total xG is at least 0.9 more than the goals scored. Quiet = after 30', total xG under 0.35 and at most one shot on target.
  • xG and xG on target are only available in leagues where SofaScore has them (most main European leagues, Brazil, MLS...). Elsewhere you still get shots, shots on target and momentum.
